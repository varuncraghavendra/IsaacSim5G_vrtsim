IsaacSim5G — running pipeline summary
=====================================

-----------------

                                                                                Subscribes /chassis/odom and bounces odom to dashboard at

What crosses the 5G radio vs. what doesn't
------------------------------------------

Stream                          Goes over the radio?   Why
------                          --------------------   ---
Teleop commands                 YES — crosses          app.py → UDP to 192.168.70.135:16000 (ext-dn) → forwarder → UPF
(W/A/S/D → Twist)               RFsim AIR              (GTP-U encap on N3) → gNB → RFsim radio → UE → oaitun_ue1:16000
                                                       → bridge.

Camera frames                   YES — crosses          viewport_to_udp.py → UDP to 192.168.70.135:17600 (ext-dn) →
(Viewport 2 → JPEG)             RFsim AIR              forwarder → UPF → gNB → RFsim radio → UE → oaitun_ue1:17600
                                                       → bridge.

Odom feedback                   NO — loopback          The bridge subscribes to the ROS topic in the same namespace as
(/chassis/odom)                                        Isaac and sends IST5 directly to 127.0.0.1:17500. Single-host
                                                       setup means "operator" = "UE host," so the dashboard is reachable
                                                       via loopback and the bridge never re-enters the radio in the
                                                       UE→gNB direction.

/cmd_vel                        NO — local ROS DDS     Stays inside ROS, never touches UDP / radio after the broker
(broker → Isaac)                                       emits it.

WebSocket browser ↔ dashboard   NO — loopback HTTP     The browser hits http://localhost:8080 directly.


A subtle point about radio direction
------------------------------------

Cellular terms have the radio's "downlink" as gNB→UE and "uplink" as UE→gNB.
In this setup both flows (commands AND camera) cross the radio in the gNB→UE
direction because the dashboard and Isaac both live on the operator side,
and we use ext-dn as the data-network ingress that feeds the UPF. The
system's "uplink" (camera feedback to dashboard) is a logical uplink in the
teleop sense, but mechanically it still rides the cellular downlink. The
cellular uplink direction (UE→gNB) is exercised by the radio's control-plane
traffic and PDCCH/PUCCH grants, not by your application data, on this
single-host topology.

Bottom line
-----------

Commands and video are genuinely traversing the OAI RFsim radio; only the
odom telemetry takes a loopback shortcut because the operator and the UE
share a machine.
